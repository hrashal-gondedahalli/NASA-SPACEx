Your flight plan

  1. Turn, then thrust. Your nose points the way. Hold a control to keep using it.
  2. Find all three gates. Follow the cyan marker. Each gate restores 12 fuel.
  3. Give debris room. Each collision costs 25% hull.
  4. Brake to dock. Enter the gold zone at speed 65 or less.

Space has no road friction. Coast to save fuel, and brake before you arrive.

Press P or Esc to pause. Flight pauses when you switch away.

Game distances, braking and flight times are simplified for learning.
