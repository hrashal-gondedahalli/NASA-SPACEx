02 / FIRST FLIGHT

Launch. Turn. Coast.

Enter Earth flight, set throttle to 100% and choose Launch guidance. It pitches east as you climb. When the booster is empty, separate it. Cut thrust when apoapsis passes 80 km. Coast near apoapsis, point prograde and burn until periapsis clears 75 km.

03 / SOFT TOUCHDOWN

The Moon has no brakes.

Moon practice starts above the surface, not after an Earth transfer. Point upward and use throttle to slow your descent. Touch down below 8 m/s with the nose within 20° of upright. No friction or magic brake in vacuum.

04 / KNOW YOUR INSTRUMENTS

Altitude isn't orbit.

Apoapsis is the highest point; periapsis is the lowest. A safe Earth orbit needs both above the atmosphere. Map shows a projected 3D coast trajectory, without thrust or drag. WASD / arrows: pitch and yaw. P: pause. X: cut engine. Space: stage.

Educational model: planets have 1/10 real radius with surface gravity retained. Three-axis position and velocity; simplified attitude, drag and flat surface contact. No interplanetary transfers, terrain, heating, docking or full rigid-body dynamics yet. Independent project; not affiliated with Spaceflight Simulator, NASA or SpaceX.
