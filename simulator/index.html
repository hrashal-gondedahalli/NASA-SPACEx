Grown-up notes

Arrows / WASD steer. P pauses, X cuts engines, Space separates a stage. Two fixed stages snap together automatically; this is not free-placement construction. Drag the blueprint to pan; use the 3D button to rotate the preview. Mouse wheel or pinch zooms.

Original SFS-inspired educational sandbox. Reduced-radius worlds, simplified attitude and drag. Moon practice starts at 1,200 m, not after an Earth transfer. No terrain, heating, docking or full rigid-body dynamics yet.
