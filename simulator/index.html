Grown-up notes

Arrows / WASD steer. P pauses, X cuts engines, Space separates a stage. Parts snap into two stages. Use ⚙ in flight to deploy landing parts and release covers or boosters. Fixed camera shows motion against the surface. Expedition automatically frames the pad and rocket together; a tracking dot marks the rocket when it becomes too small to see. Reach orbit starts at normal speed; use the time control to speed it up. Gold map lines predict engines-off motion; cyan records the flown path. Moon descent starts in a fixed wide view; the rocket is enlarged as a visual marker. Zoom keeps your chosen camera mode. Follow deliberately centres the rocket. Tap the camera button for Follow or Free; flight zoom focuses your rocket. Drag the blueprint to pan; use the 3D button to rotate the preview. Mouse wheel or pinch zooms.

Expedition adds one continuous Earth–Moon journey with Astronomy Engine ephemerides, a finite-thrust transfer assistant and gravity from Earth, Moon and differential solar attraction. All lengths are 1:10; model time advances by √0.1 of astronomical time. Advanced mode uses manual flight. Solar-system map positions are date-based; Mars and other planets are map destinations only in this release. Surface contact remains spherical; Rapier, walkable terrain and astronaut EVA are not yet included.

Original SFS-inspired educational sandbox. Reduced-radius worlds, simplified attitude and drag. Moon descent starts at 1,200 m; lunar orbit practice starts at 30 km. These practice starts are separate scenarios. Choose Expedition for continuous Earth-to-Moon flight. Map lets you inspect both destinations and the Moon’s elliptical orbit. Sizes and distances use the same 1:10 model scale; the Moon orbit has a model-scaled period and arbitrary starting phase, not a live ephemeris. Its 5.145° tilt is relative to the display’s ecliptic reference plane. Gold shows the current unpowered two-body trajectory, not a guided route. Heating and recovery are simplified; no terrain collision meshes, docking or full rigid-body dynamics.

Moon surface texture: Three.js examples. Mean lunar orbit parameters: NASA Moon Fact Sheet. In practice scenarios the companion is a visual reference. Expedition includes Earth, Moon and differential solar gravity. See imagery credits.
