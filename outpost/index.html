    TODAY’S MISSION

    SUPPLY LOCKER

    Think about tomorrow

    Every day: −18 oxygen, −12 water and −12 food. Each greenhouse level also uses 3 water. Stores below 20 reduce crew health; zero triggers a safe evacuation.

    How to keep your crew safe

    Try oxygen 2, water recycling 1 and greenhouse 1. Watch the next-day preview, repair damaged panels and shelter on alert day.

    Plants take one game day to grow. Today’s greenhouse settings make tomorrow’s food. Shelter pauses growth, so save food in advance.

    A simulated day is a decision round, not a model of the Moon’s day/night cycle. Our fictional site has a daily solar supply; battery capacity is 60 units.
